Informed Consent Form

About this study: You are invited to take part in evaluating a software tool that retrieves step-by-step illustrated instructions in response to a typed query (for example, ``brush teeth''). The system is being developed as part of a graduation thesis project and is intended, in the long term, to support users with autism spectrum disorder. This pilot is a first-stage evaluation with neurotypical adults; testing with the target user group is planned separately. Please read this form carefully and decide whether to participate.

What you'll do: The session lasts only a few minutes and consists of: Using the website freely to search for instructions on tasks of your choice. Completing a short questionnaire about your experience after getting the results from the questions you asked.

Your data: We collect your email, age, gender, highest education in the post task questionnaire.

Voluntary: Participation is completely voluntary. You may stop at any time.

Contact: If you have any questions or concerns about this study, please contact the researcher at c.zhang7@student.vu.nl or chizhang119@gmail.com before proceeding.
